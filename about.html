Logistics organized around people.

TOJDORON is an international transport company that brings route planning, cargo movement, and practical support together in one place.

We help cargo move worldwide.

We offer comprehensive logistics solutions for businesses and private clients across road, sea, cargo, and air freight.

Our role starts before the shipment moves. We learn what is being transported, where it needs to go, how quickly it needs to arrive, and which handling conditions matter. From there, we organize the transport option that fits.

As the cargo moves, TOJDORON monitors the important transit stages and remains available as a clear point of contact. That continuity is at the heart of our work.
