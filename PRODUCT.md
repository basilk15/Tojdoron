# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Each page has its own HTML, CSS, and JavaScript files. No React, Next.js, or other application framework.

## Users

- Confirmed: businesses and private clients that need international cargo transportation.
- Inferred from the brief: prospective clients evaluating a freight partner and seeking a fast way to request a quote.

## Product Purpose

TOJDORON presents and coordinates international freight services across road, sea, air, and flexible cargo shipping. The website should help a visitor understand the available transport modes, trust the company to coordinate the shipment, and contact the Dushanbe office for a quote.

## Positioning

TOJDORON is presented as a one-stop logistics partner that selects the appropriate transport mode, monitors transit, and supports cargo through each delivery stage.

## Operating Context

Visitors may be comparing transport options, planning a commercial shipment, or arranging private cargo. They need clear service explanations, the Dushanbe office location, direct phone and email access, and a concise quote-request form.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Four pages: Home, About Us, Services, and Contact Us.
- Services: Road Freight, Sea Freight, Cargo Shipping, and Air Freight.
- Quote and contact calls to action route to the Contact page.
- Contact page includes a form and a Google map pinned to the supplied Dushanbe address.
- The initial site language is inferred to be English from the supplied company copy.
- No form-processing backend was supplied; the static build will validate locally and prepare an email enquiry rather than claim server-side delivery.

## Product Principles

- Make international freight options easy to compare.
- Put a clear human contact path near every decision point.
- Use concrete transport imagery while keeping all brand claims honest.
- Present TOJDORON as globally capable and locally reachable in Dushanbe.
- Keep the static implementation lightweight, responsive, and maintainable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic landmarks, keyboard-operable navigation and controls, visible focus states, readable contrast, form labels with local error feedback, meaningful image alternatives, reduced-motion support, and responsive layouts without horizontal scrolling.
